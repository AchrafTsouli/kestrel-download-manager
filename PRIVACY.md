# Privacy

Flux and its browser extension do not collect, send or sell any personal data.

**The Flux app** keeps its settings, download history and saved site logins on your computer, encrypted for your Windows account. It connects only to:

- the sites you download from;
- GitHub, to check for Flux updates (when enabled in Options) and newer versions of the video downloader yt-dlp;
- the peers and trackers of torrents you add.

**The browser extension** talks only to the Flux app on the same computer, through the browser's native messaging. When it hands over a download or video you chose, it passes:

- the address;
- the file name;
- the page it came from;
- for sites that need a signed-in session, the cookies and request headers for that one site.

Flux uses them for that download only.

Nothing is sent to the developers or to anyone else. There are no analytics, no advertising and no accounts.
